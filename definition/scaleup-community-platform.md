# ScaleUP — Community Platform Module

> ScaleUP is a Business Operating System and Business Community Platform developed by **ZentreLink**. This document specifies the Community Platform module — the free, always-available experience every registered user receives. For the full product vision, business model, and platform architecture, see [`docs/`](../../docs/README.md), particularly [PRODUCT_VISION.md](../../docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md) and [PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md](../../docs/PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md).

## 1. Community Platform

### Overview
The Community Platform serves as the social and professional networking layer of ScaleUP. It enables individuals to build their professional presence, connect with other entrepreneurs and organizations, share business-related content, discover opportunities, and engage with a growing business ecosystem.

Unlike traditional social media, the Community Platform focuses exclusively on business growth, collaboration, knowledge sharing, and professional networking.

### Objectives
- Build a professional business community.
- Encourage collaboration between entrepreneurs and organizations.
- Share business knowledge and experiences.
- Discover new business opportunities.
- Create meaningful professional connections.

---

## 2. Individual Account

### Overview
An Individual Account represents a personal identity within the ScaleUP platform. Every user begins as an individual and gains access to community features without requiring an organization.

Individuals can participate in discussions, build professional connections, explore opportunities, and later create or join organizations.

### Objectives
- Provide every user with a professional identity.
- Allow users to participate in the business community.
- Enable networking and collaboration.
- Support future organization ownership or membership.

---

## 3. User Profile

### Overview
The User Profile showcases a user's professional identity, experience, interests, achievements, and business activities. It acts as a digital business profile that helps establish credibility and build professional relationships.

### Objectives
- Present a professional identity.
- Highlight business experience and expertise.
- Build trust within the community.
- Support networking opportunities.

---

## 4. Community Feed

### Overview
The Community Feed is the central activity stream of ScaleUP where users and organizations share updates, business insights, achievements, opportunities, and discussions.

The feed is designed to encourage knowledge sharing and professional engagement while keeping content focused on business-related topics.

### Objectives
- Share business updates.
- Promote meaningful discussions.
- Encourage collaboration.
- Surface relevant business content.
- Keep users informed about community activities.

---

## 5. Business Connections

### Overview
Business Connections allow individuals and organizations to establish professional relationships within the ScaleUP ecosystem.

Connections help expand professional networks, encourage collaboration, and create new business opportunities.

### Objectives
- Build trusted business relationships.
- Expand professional networks.
- Support collaboration between users and organizations.

---

## 6. Organizations

### Overview
Organizations represent businesses, companies, startups, or professional groups within ScaleUP.

Organizations provide a dedicated workspace where members collaborate, manage operations, monitor performance, and achieve business goals.

Creating an organization unlocks ScaleUP's business management capabilities through a subscription plan.

### Objectives
- Represent a business within the platform.
- Provide a collaborative workspace.
- Enable business management features.
- Support team-based operations.

---

## 7. Organization Workspace

### Overview
The Organization Workspace is the dedicated business environment for subscribed organizations. It provides tools for managing operations, monitoring business performance, planning growth, collaborating with team members, and accessing business intelligence.

Unlike the Community Platform, the Organization Workspace focuses on internal business management and organizational productivity.

### Objectives
- Centralize business operations.
- Improve team collaboration.
- Support data-driven decision-making.
- Enable long-term business growth.

---

## 8. Subscription Management

### Overview
Subscription Management controls access to premium organization features within ScaleUP.

While individual accounts can access the Community Platform for free, organizations require an active subscription to use business management tools and collaborative workspaces.

### Objectives
- Manage organization subscriptions.
- Control access to premium features.
- Support scalable subscription plans.
- Simplify billing and renewals.

---

## 9. Opportunities

### Overview
The Opportunities module enables individuals and organizations to discover and share business opportunities such as partnerships, investments, collaborations, hiring, and strategic initiatives.

This module aims to connect businesses with the resources and relationships needed for sustainable growth.

### Objectives
- Connect businesses with opportunities.
- Encourage partnerships and collaboration.
- Increase visibility for business initiatives.
- Support long-term business expansion.

---

## Platform Architecture

```
ScaleUP

Community Platform (Free)
│
├── Individual Account
├── User Profile
├── Community Feed
├── Business Connections
├── Messaging
├── Notifications
└── Opportunities

Organization Workspace (Subscription)
│
├── Organization
├── Business Dashboard
├── Business Management
├── Financial Management
├── Planning
├── Analytics
├── Reports
├── Team Management
└── Organization Settings
```

## Core Principle

ScaleUP is built on two interconnected experiences:

- **Community Platform** — A free professional business network where individuals can connect, share, and discover opportunities.
- **Organization Workspace** — A subscription-based business operating environment where organizations manage their operations, collaborate with teams, and drive sustainable growth.
