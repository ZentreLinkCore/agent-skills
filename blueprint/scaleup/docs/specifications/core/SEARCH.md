# Search Specification

## Purpose

The Search module provides a unified way for users to discover people, organizations, posts, and opportunities across the ScaleUP platform.

Its purpose is to make the ScaleUP ecosystem discoverable while respecting privacy, visibility, and authorization rules.

---

## Overview

Search operates across the Community Platform and returns only the results a user is permitted to see.

Search results must respect user privacy, organization visibility, permissions, and content access rules.

---

## Goals

- Help users quickly discover relevant information.
- Support professional and business discovery.
- Provide consistent search across ScaleUP.
- Respect privacy and authorization rules.

---

## 1. Search Scope

Search may cover:

- Users
- Organizations
- Posts
- Opportunities
- Public content
- Organization members where permitted

---

## 2. Global Search

The global search experience may provide:

```text
Search ScaleUP

People
Organizations
Posts
Opportunities
```

Results should be grouped by entity type.

---

## 3. User Search

Users may search for people using:

- Name
- Headline
- Industry
- Skills
- Location
- Professional interests

Only discoverable profiles should appear.

---

## 4. Organization Search

Users may search organizations using:

- Organization name
- Industry
- Location
- Organization type
- Public description

Private organizations or restricted information must not be exposed.

---

## 5. Post Search

Users may discover publicly accessible posts using:

- Keywords
- Topics
- Author
- Organization
- Date

Search results must respect post visibility and access restrictions.

---

## 6. Opportunity Search

Users may search for opportunities using:

- Opportunity type
- Industry
- Location
- Keywords
- Organization
- Status

Possible opportunity types:

- Partnership
- Investment
- Collaboration
- Mentorship
- Hiring
- Project
- Business Service
- Event

---

## 7. Search Filters

### People

- Industry
- Location
- Skills

### Organizations

- Industry
- Location
- Organization Type

### Posts

- Topic
- Author
- Organization
- Date

### Opportunities

- Type
- Industry
- Location
- Status

---

## 8. Search Suggestions

The search interface may provide suggestions while the user types.

Example:

```text
Search:
"coffee"

Suggestions:

Organizations
- ABC Coffee

People
- John Coffee

Posts
- Coffee Business Growth

Opportunities
- Coffee Shop Partnership
```

---

## 9. Search History

### Status

Future capability.

Users may be able to:

- View recent searches
- Clear search history
- Disable search history

---

## 10. Search Permissions

Search must respect:

- Profile visibility
- Organization visibility
- Post visibility
- Opportunity visibility
- User blocking
- Organization access
- Subscription-related restrictions where applicable

---

## 11. Search Principles

#### Relevant

Prioritize useful and meaningful results.

#### Discoverable

Help users discover people, organizations, and opportunities.

#### Private

Never expose information the user is not authorized to access.

#### Consistent

Search behavior and filtering should remain consistent across ScaleUP.
