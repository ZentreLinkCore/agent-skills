# Mentorship Specification

## Purpose

The Mentorship module is a future ScaleUP capability designed to connect entrepreneurs and business professionals with experienced mentors who can provide guidance, knowledge, and strategic support.

---

## Overview

Mentorship extends the ScaleUP Community by creating structured relationships between users seeking guidance and individuals or organizations willing to provide expertise.

---

## Actors

- Mentee
- Mentor
- Organization
- Platform Administrator

---

## Potential Capabilities

- Mentor Profiles
- Expertise Areas
- Mentorship Requests
- Mentor Discovery
- Matching
- Sessions
- Scheduling
- Reviews
- Mentorship Programs

---

## Potential Flow

```text
Mentor Creates Profile
    ↓
Defines Expertise
    ↓
Mentee Searches / Gets Matched
    ↓
Mentorship Request
    ↓
Mentor Accepts
    ↓
Mentorship Relationship
    ↓
Sessions
    ↓
Progress / Feedback
```

---

## Rules

- Mentors should clearly represent their expertise and experience.
- Users should not be guaranteed business outcomes through mentorship.
- Mentorship relationships must respect privacy.
- Platform policies should define appropriate conduct.
- Paid mentorship capabilities must use a separate commercial policy if introduced.

---

## Future Enhancements

- AI mentor matching
- Mentorship programs
- Group mentorship
- Business coaching
- Expert consultations
- Scheduling integrations

---

## Success Criteria

Users should be able to discover qualified mentors and establish meaningful professional relationships that support business and personal development.
