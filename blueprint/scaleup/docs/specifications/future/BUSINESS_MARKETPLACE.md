# Business Marketplace Specification

## Purpose

The Business Marketplace is a future ScaleUP capability designed to connect organizations with businesses, service providers, products, and resources that can help them operate and grow.

---

## Overview

The Marketplace extends ScaleUP from business management and networking into business-to-business commerce and service discovery.

Potential marketplace categories include:

- Business Services
- Software
- Professional Services
- Suppliers
- Equipment
- Marketing
- Consulting
- Recruitment
- Logistics

---

## Actors

- Buyer Organization
- Seller Organization
- Service Provider
- Individual Professional
- Platform Administrator

---

## Potential Capabilities

- Marketplace Listings
- Business Services
- Product Listings
- Provider Profiles
- Search and Discovery
- Reviews
- Requests
- Business Inquiries
- Orders
- Payments

---

## Potential Flow

```text
Provider
    ↓
Create Listing
    ↓
Publish
    ↓
Organization Discovery
    ↓
View Listing
    ↓
Contact / Request
    ↓
Agreement / Transaction
```

---

## Rules

- Marketplace listings must meet platform standards.
- Businesses must provide accurate information.
- Fraudulent or misleading listings must be subject to moderation.
- Payment processing requires approved payment infrastructure.
- Reviews must follow platform integrity rules.
- Marketplace functionality must remain separate from private organization business data.

---

## Future Enhancements

- Business-to-business purchasing
- Service contracts
- Verified providers
- Marketplace payments
- Reviews and ratings
- Recommendations
- Business procurement workflows

---

## Success Criteria

The Marketplace should help organizations discover trusted products, services, suppliers, and professional resources that can support business operations and growth.
