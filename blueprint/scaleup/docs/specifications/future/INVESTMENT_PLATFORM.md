# Investment Platform Specification

## Purpose

The Investment Platform is a future ScaleUP capability designed to connect qualified businesses seeking capital with potential investors and funding partners.

Its purpose is to create a structured environment where businesses can present opportunities, communicate their growth potential, and connect with potential sources of capital.

---

## Overview

The Investment Platform builds on ScaleUP's existing:

- Business Profiles
- Organization Profiles
- Business Data
- Business Planning
- Analytics
- Opportunities
- Community

The platform may eventually allow organizations to publish investment opportunities and allow eligible users or organizations to discover and evaluate them.

---

## Rules

- Investment opportunities must be subject to verification and platform policies.
- Financial claims must not be presented as guaranteed outcomes.
- Private business information must only be shared with authorized participants.
- Investment functionality must comply with applicable laws and regulations in supported markets.
- ScaleUP must clearly define whether it is acting as a marketplace, intermediary, technology provider, or another role.
- Actual investment transactions must not be enabled without appropriate legal, compliance, security, and payment infrastructure.
