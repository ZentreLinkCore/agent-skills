# Design Principles

These principles guide every product decision made across ScaleUP's two experiences. They exist so that as the platform grows — new modules, new features, new teams — the product stays coherent with its vision.

---

## 1. Community First

Every user is a person before they are a business. The Community Platform is the default experience for every registered user, and it must always stand on its own as valuable — never designed merely as a waiting room for the Organization Workspace.

---

## 2. Organizations Are Opt-In, Not Required

Creating an Organization is a deliberate decision driven by business need, not a milestone the product pushes users toward. Features and messaging should never imply that an Individual is "incomplete" without one.

---

## 3. A Clear Line Between Free and Subscription

The boundary between the Community Platform and the Organization Workspace must always be legible to users: what is free, what requires a subscription, and why. Ambiguity at this boundary erodes trust in the platform's value proposition on both sides.

---

## 4. One Identity, Two Contexts

A user's professional identity — profile, network, reputation — is singular and persists across both experiences. The Organization Workspace is an additional context layered on top of that identity, never a separate account or a fractured experience.

---

## 5. Business-Focused Content, Always

The Community Platform is not a general social network. Every feature — feed, discussions, opportunities, connections — exists to serve business growth, professional development, and entrepreneurial collaboration. Content and interactions should be evaluated against that purpose.

---

## 6. Operate on Data, Not Guesswork

Inside the Organization Workspace, every module — Dashboard, Reports, Analytics, Planning — should help a business see itself clearly and decide deliberately. Business management tools should default to showing the data behind a decision, not just the decision itself.

---

## 7. Progressive Depth

New users should never be confronted with the full complexity of business management before they need it. Complexity — Organizations, Planning, Analytics, Team Management — should reveal itself progressively, as a business's needs grow.

---

## 8. Built to Extend

The platform must accommodate new business modules (see [specifications/future](./specifications/future/)) without disrupting the core experience. Architecture and design decisions should favor extensibility within the Organization Workspace over one-off, tightly coupled features.

---

## 9. Ecosystem Value Over Individual Feature Value

When evaluating a new feature, prioritize its effect on the health of the overall ecosystem — network density, opportunity flow, organization retention — over its standalone usage metrics. ScaleUP's value compounds through the interaction of individuals, organizations, and opportunities, not through any single feature in isolation.
