# Product Roadmap

This roadmap describes the phased direction of ScaleUP as it matures from a business networking product into a full Business Operating System. Phases are organized by capability, not by fixed dates, and later phases build on the foundation established by earlier ones.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation: Community & Identity

Establish the Community Platform as a standalone, valuable product for every registered user.

- Individual Accounts and professional profiles
- Community Feed for business updates and discussions
- Business Connections and networking
- Messaging and Notifications
- Opportunity discovery

**Goal:** Every user gets real value from ScaleUP without ever creating an Organization.

---

## Phase 2 — Activation: Organizations & Workspace

Introduce the Organization Workspace as the subscription-based layer for businesses ready to formalize operations.

- Organization creation and subscription management
- Business Dashboard
- Core Business Management tools
- Team Management and Organization Settings

**Goal:** Give businesses a reason to convert from Individual to Organization, backed by real operational value.

---

## Phase 3 — Depth: Planning, Reporting & Analytics

Deepen the Organization Workspace with the tools businesses need to plan and measure growth deliberately.

- Financial Management (Sales, Expenses, Transactions)
- Reports
- Analytics
- Business Planning
- Goals & KPIs

**Goal:** Move organizations from simply operating on ScaleUP to actively planning and measuring growth through it.

---

## Phase 4 — Expansion: Ecosystem & Future Modules

Strengthen the connective tissue between the Community Platform and the Organization Workspace, and extend the workspace with new business modules.

- Deeper integration between community activity and organization presence
- Expanded opportunity types (partnerships, investment, hiring)
- Additional business modules, incubated under [specifications/future](./specifications/future/)

**Goal:** Compound the value of the ecosystem — more organizations, more opportunities, more reasons for individuals and businesses to stay engaged.

---

## Guiding Constraint

At every phase, the Community Platform remains free and the Organization Workspace remains subscription-based. New capability is added by deepening each experience, not by shifting the line between them. See [ADR-002: Subscription Model](./decisions/ADR-002-subscription-model.md).
