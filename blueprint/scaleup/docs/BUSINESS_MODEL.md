# Business Model

ScaleUP's business model mirrors its product structure: the Community Platform is free and open, while the Organization Workspace is subscription-based. This keeps the network large and valuable while monetizing the tools businesses need once they choose to formalize and operate.

---

## Individual Accounts — Free

Every registered user is, by default, an Individual. There is no paid tier for individual participation in the Community Platform.

**Included:**
- Community access
- Professional networking
- Business feed
- Messaging
- Connections
- Opportunity discovery

**Why it's free:** The value of the Community Platform comes from the size and diversity of its network — professionals, entrepreneurs, investors, and mentors. A free, unrestricted Individual tier maximizes participation, which in turn makes the ecosystem more valuable for every organization operating within it.

---

## Organizations — Subscription-Based

Organizations are optional. A user only creates an Organization when they intend to manage a business, and doing so requires an active subscription.

**Unlocked by subscription:**
- Business Workspace
- Business Management
- Analytics
- Planning
- Reports
- Team Collaboration

**Why it's paid:** The Organization Workspace delivers operational value directly to a business's bottom line — running operations, planning growth, and making data-driven decisions. Organizations are willing to pay for tools that materially improve how they manage and scale, in the same way they already pay for other business-critical software.

---

## The Relationship Between the Two Tiers

The free Community Platform is not a funnel built solely to convert users into paying subscribers — it is a durable, valuable product in its own right. The Organization Workspace is a natural next step for the subset of users who are ready to formalize and operate a business, but individuals who never create an Organization remain full participants in the ecosystem.

This structure gives ScaleUP two reinforcing growth loops:

1. **Community growth** — free access drives network size, engagement, and the discovery of business opportunities.
2. **Workspace growth** — as organizations join to manage their business, they bring their teams, partners, and activity back into the community, further strengthening the network.

As the Organization Workspace matures, additional business modules (see [specifications/future](./specifications/future/)) extend the value delivered within the subscription, deepening the reason for organizations to stay — and grow — on ScaleUP.
