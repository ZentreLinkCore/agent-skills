### 1.5 Scope and Limitations

#### 1.5.1 Scope

The proposed system will focus on developing a social music and community platform that integrates communication, community interaction, music discovery, and synchronized group listening. The initial implementation will focus on the web application, while the backend architecture will be designed to support future mobile and desktop applications.

The system will include the following functionalities:

1. **User Authentication and Account Management**
   Users will be able to register, log in, log out, manage their profiles, and update basic account information.

2. **Direct Messaging**
   Users will be able to communicate privately through one-on-one conversations with support for real-time message delivery.

3. **Group Messaging**
   Users will be able to participate in group conversations where multiple members can communicate within a shared conversation.

4. **Interactive Messaging Features**
   The messaging system will support features such as message reactions, replies, threads, mentions, message editing, message deletion, typing indicators, read states, and online presence where applicable.

5. **Community Management**
   Users will be able to create or join communities and participate in organized channels or discussion spaces. Community-related features may include member management, channel management, and basic moderation controls.

6. **Music Discovery and Playback**
   Users will be able to search and browse music made available through an authorized third-party music provider. The system will display relevant music information and provide playback functionality according to the capabilities and restrictions of the selected provider.

7. **Jam Shared Listening**
   Users will be able to create, join, and leave Jam sessions. Participants will be able to listen to the same music session while playback actions such as play, pause, track changes, and seeking are synchronized through real-time communication.

8. **Real-Time Communication**
   The system will use real-time communication technologies for features that require immediate updates, including messaging, typing indicators, presence information, notifications, and Jam playback synchronization.

9. **Notifications**
   The system will provide notifications for relevant user activities, such as new messages, conversation interactions, community activities, and Jam-related events.

10. **Security and Access Control**
    The system will implement authentication, authorization, input validation, access control, secure API communication, rate limiting where applicable, and protection of sensitive credentials.

11. **API-First Backend**
    The backend will expose structured APIs that can be consumed by the web application and potentially reused by future mobile and desktop applications.

#### 1.5.2 Limitations

The proposed system will have the following limitations:

1. **Music Provider Dependency**
   Music availability, search functionality, playback capabilities, regional availability, API limits, and other restrictions will depend on the selected third-party music provider.

2. **Music Licensing and Distribution Restrictions**
   The system will not independently host, copy, download, or redistribute copyrighted music unless the necessary rights and licenses are available. Music playback will be implemented according to the terms and technical capabilities of the selected provider.

3. **Jam Synchronization Limitations**
   Playback synchronization will depend on network conditions, device performance, latency, browser behavior, and the capabilities of the music provider. Minor differences in playback position may occur between participants.

4. **Initial Web Platform Focus**
   The initial implementation will focus on the web application. Native mobile and desktop applications will be considered future extensions of the system.

5. **No Direct Music Uploading in the Initial Version**
   Users will not be able to upload and distribute their own commercial music files through the platform during the initial implementation.

6. **No Offline Music Downloads**
   The initial system will not provide functionality for downloading music for offline playback.

7. **No Built-in Voice or Video Communication in the Initial Version**
   Voice calls, video calls, screen sharing, and other real-time media communication features are outside the initial scope. These may be considered for future versions.

8. **Limited Recommendation Features**
   Advanced music recommendation algorithms, personalized machine-learning recommendations, and automated content discovery will not be part of the initial implementation.

9. **Limited Monetization Features**
   Subscription systems, advertisements, creator monetization, payment processing, and other commercial features will not be included in the initial version unless they become part of a future development phase.

10. **Third-Party Service Availability**
    Some system functionality may depend on external services such as music providers and real-time communication providers. Service interruptions, API changes, rate limits, or changes in third-party policies may affect the corresponding functionality.

The defined scope and limitations are intended to keep the initial development focused on the core purpose of the system while providing an architecture that can accommodate additional functionality in future versions.
