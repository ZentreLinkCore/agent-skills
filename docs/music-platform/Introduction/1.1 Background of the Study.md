### 1.1 Background of the Study

The rapid growth of digital communication and online communities has changed the way people interact, collaborate, and consume digital content. Messaging applications, community platforms, and music streaming services have become part of users' everyday digital activities. However, these experiences are commonly distributed across different applications, requiring users to switch between platforms depending on whether they want to communicate, participate in a community, discover music, or listen to music with others.

Most existing communication platforms provide features such as direct messaging, group conversations, channels, reactions, threads, and notifications. Similarly, music streaming platforms primarily focus on individual music discovery and playback. Although some platforms provide social features, shared music experiences are often limited or dependent on external services. This separation creates an opportunity for a platform that integrates communication, community interaction, music discovery, and synchronized listening into a single system.

The proposed system, **[Project Name] — Social Music & Community Platform**, is designed as a cross-platform application that combines real-time communication, community-based interaction, music discovery, and synchronized group listening. The system will allow users to communicate through direct and group messages, participate in communities and channels, discover available music, and create or join a shared listening session called **Jam**.

One of the main features of the proposed system is the **Jam** functionality. A Jam allows multiple users to listen to the same music session while maintaining synchronized playback. Actions such as playing, pausing, changing tracks, and seeking to a different position can be synchronized among participants through real-time communication technology. Instead of transmitting the actual music audio through the application's communication server, the system will coordinate playback states while allowing clients to obtain the music through an authorized music provider.

The system is intended to follow an API-first and modular architecture so that the same backend services can support multiple client applications. The initial implementation will focus on the web platform, while the architecture will allow future development of mobile and desktop applications. This approach enables the system to evolve while maintaining a common authentication, messaging, community, music, and Jam infrastructure.

Through the integration of these functionalities, the proposed system aims to provide a unified environment where users can communicate, participate in communities, discover music, and experience music together in real time.
