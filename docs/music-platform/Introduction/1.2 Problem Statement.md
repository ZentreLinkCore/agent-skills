### 1.2 Problem Statement

The increasing use of digital communication and music streaming platforms has provided users with convenient ways to communicate, participate in online communities, and access music. However, these activities are often separated across different applications and services. Users may need to switch between messaging platforms, community applications, and music streaming services to perform activities that are related to one another.

The following problems are identified:

1. **Fragmented Communication and Music Experiences**
   Users commonly rely on separate applications for messaging, community interaction, and music streaming. This separation can make it difficult to maintain communication and shared activities within a single environment.

2. **Limited Integration of Music into Social Communication**
   Existing communication platforms may allow users to share music links or references, but the music experience is often handled outside the communication platform. This limits the ability of users to interact around music without leaving the current conversation or community.

3. **Lack of Synchronized Group Listening**
   Individual music playback does not necessarily provide a coordinated listening experience for multiple users. When users attempt to listen to the same music together, differences in playback position, play, pause, or seeking actions can cause the listening experience to become unsynchronized.

4. **Limited Real-Time Interaction During Shared Activities**
   Messaging, presence, typing indicators, reactions, and shared music playback require real-time communication between users. Without an appropriate real-time mechanism, changes made by one user may not be immediately reflected for other participants.

5. **Fragmented Community and Group Interaction**
   Users participating in groups or communities may need separate platforms for discussions, channels, private conversations, and other forms of social interaction. A unified system could provide a more consistent environment for these activities.

6. **Challenges in Supporting Multiple Platforms**
   Developing separate systems for web, mobile, and desktop applications can result in duplicated backend logic and inconsistent functionality. A centralized and reusable backend architecture is therefore needed to support multiple client applications while maintaining consistent system behavior.

To address these problems, the proposed system will integrate real-time messaging, community interaction, music discovery, and synchronized group listening into a single platform. The system will use a centralized backend and real-time communication architecture to coordinate interactions between users while maintaining a modular structure that can support future web, mobile, and desktop applications.
