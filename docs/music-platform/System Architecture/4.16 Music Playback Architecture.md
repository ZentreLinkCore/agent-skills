## 4.16 Music Playback Architecture

Music playback shall primarily occur on the client when supported by the selected provider.

The architecture shall therefore separate:

```text
Music Catalog
       │
       ▼
Music Provider

Playback
       │
       ▼
Client Application

Playback Synchronization
       │
       ▼
Jam Service + Realtime Provider
```

This separation prevents the Laravel backend from becoming an unnecessary audio-streaming server.

It also allows the Jam service to synchronize playback state without carrying the actual music stream.
