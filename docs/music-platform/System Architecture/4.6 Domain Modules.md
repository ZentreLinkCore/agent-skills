## 4.6 Domain Modules

The backend shall be organized around major business domains.

Recommended domains include:

```text
Auth
Messaging
Groups
Community
Music
Jam
Notification
Search
File
Settings
Administration
Audit
```

Each domain shall contain its own relevant business rules, services, requests, policies, events, and supporting components where appropriate.

A conceptual Laravel structure may be:

```text
app/
├── Domain/
│   ├── Auth/
│   ├── Messaging/
│   ├── Groups/
│   ├── Community/
│   ├── Music/
│   ├── Jam/
│   ├── Notification/
│   ├── Search/
│   ├── File/
│   ├── Settings/
│   ├── Administration/
│   └── Audit/
│
├── Http/
│   ├── Controllers/
│   ├── Requests/
│   └── Resources/
│
├── Events/
├── Jobs/
├── Models/
├── Policies/
└── Providers/
```

The exact folder structure may be adjusted during implementation as long as the architectural separation remains clear.
