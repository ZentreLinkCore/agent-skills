## 4.14 Community Architecture

Communities shall provide organized spaces for users to interact through channels.

The hierarchy shall generally be:

```text
Community
   │
   ├── Members
   │
   ├── Roles
   │
   └── Channels
          │
          ├── General
          ├── Announcements
          ├── Music
          └── Other Channels
```

Community permissions shall be separate from system-wide permissions.

A user may therefore be:

```text
Community A → Administrator
Community B → Moderator
Community C → Member
```
