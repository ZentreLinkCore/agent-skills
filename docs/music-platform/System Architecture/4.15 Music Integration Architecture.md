## 4.15 Music Integration Architecture

The music subsystem shall use a provider abstraction layer.

The application shall not make the rest of the system directly dependent on one specific music provider.

The architecture shall be:

```text
                    ┌──────────────────┐
                    │  Music Service   │
                    └────────┬─────────┘
                             │
                    Provider Adapter
                             │
             ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
             ▼               ▼               ▼
       Music Provider A Music Provider B Music Provider C
```

The provider abstraction shall normalize information such as:

* Track ID
* Track title
* Artist
* Album
* Artwork
* Duration
* Provider
* Playback availability
* Provider URL
* Additional provider metadata where permitted

The system shall store only information that is permitted by the selected provider's API terms and applicable licensing requirements.

The backend shall not download, copy, or redistribute protected music audio unless explicitly authorized and legally permitted.
