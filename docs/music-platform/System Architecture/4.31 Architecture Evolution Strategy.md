## 4.31 Architecture Evolution Strategy

The initial architecture shall prioritize simplicity and maintainability rather than prematurely dividing the system into many independent microservices.

The initial system shall preferably operate as a modular Laravel application.

```text
                Laravel Application
                       │
       ┌───────────────┼────────────────┐
       │               │                │
   Messaging       Community          Music
       │               │                │
       └───────────────┼────────────────┘
                       │
                      Jam
```

As the platform grows, specific components may be extracted only when there is a demonstrated need.

Potential future service boundaries include:

* Realtime service
* Search service
* Media processing service
* Notification service
* Music integration service
* Analytics service

This approach avoids introducing unnecessary operational complexity during the MVP stage.
