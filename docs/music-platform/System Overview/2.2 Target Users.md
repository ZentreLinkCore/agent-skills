### 2.2 Target Users

The proposed system is designed for individuals and groups who want to communicate, participate in communities, discover music, and engage in shared listening experiences. The system will support different types of users based on their responsibilities and level of access.

#### 2.2.1 Regular User

The Regular User is the primary user of the platform. Regular users can create and manage their accounts, communicate with other users, participate in communities, discover music, and join Jam sessions.

The main activities of a Regular User include:

* Registering and managing an account
* Creating and updating a user profile
* Sending and receiving direct messages
* Participating in group conversations
* Reacting to and replying to messages
* Joining communities and channels
* Searching and discovering available music
* Creating or joining Jam sessions
* Participating in synchronized music listening
* Managing personal notifications and preferences

#### 2.2.2 Community Administrator

The Community Administrator is responsible for managing a specific community. This user has additional permissions compared with regular community members.

The main responsibilities of a Community Administrator include:

* Creating and managing community information
* Creating and managing community channels
* Managing community members
* Controlling member access where applicable
* Managing community-related activities
* Performing basic moderation functions
* Managing community-specific settings

Community administrators will only have administrative permissions within communities where they have been granted the appropriate role or permission.

#### 2.2.3 System Administrator

The System Administrator is responsible for managing the overall platform and its system-level resources. This role has broader permissions than community administrators.

The main responsibilities of a System Administrator include:

* Managing user accounts
* Managing system-level configurations
* Monitoring system resources and activities
* Managing platform-wide settings
* Managing reported content or users where applicable
* Managing system access and administrative permissions
* Monitoring the availability and operation of major system services

System administrators will have access to administrative functions that are not available to regular users or community administrators.

#### 2.2.4 Jam Host

The Jam Host is a user who creates and controls a Jam session. The Jam Host may be a regular user but receives additional permissions within a specific Jam session.

The Jam Host may be able to:

* Create a Jam session
* Invite or allow other users to join
* Select the current track
* Control playback
* Pause or resume the session
* Change the current track
* Seek to a different playback position
* End the Jam session

The permissions available to the Jam Host may be configurable depending on the final Jam design.

#### 2.2.5 Jam Participant

A Jam Participant is a user who joins an existing Jam session. Participants can listen to the shared music session and receive synchronized playback updates from the Jam.

Depending on the permissions configured by the Jam Host, participants may also be allowed to perform certain playback actions, such as requesting or controlling playback changes.

#### 2.2.6 Future User Roles

The system architecture will allow additional roles and permission levels to be introduced in future versions. Potential roles may include moderators, content creators, music curators, community managers, or other specialized roles depending on the future requirements of the platform.

The role-based structure will allow the system to control access to features and resources while maintaining separation between regular user activities, community management, and system administration.
