### 3.5 Communities and Channels

The Communities and Channels module will provide organized spaces where users can participate in discussions, share information, interact with other members, and engage in community activities. A community will serve as a higher-level container that may contain multiple channels, members, roles, and permissions.

Unlike private group conversations, communities are intended to support larger groups and organized discussions based on shared interests, activities, or purposes.

#### 3.5.1 Community Creation

The system shall allow authorized users to create a community where community creation is enabled.

When creating a community, the creator may provide:

* Community name
* Community description
* Community image or icon
* Community visibility
* Initial settings

The creator shall become the initial community owner or administrator.

#### 3.5.2 Community Membership

Users shall be able to join communities according to the community's configured access rules.

A community may use different membership methods, including:

* Open membership
* Invitation-based membership
* Approval-based membership
* Other supported access mechanisms

The system shall maintain a membership record for each user participating in a community.

#### 3.5.3 Community Roles

The system shall support role-based permissions within communities.

Possible community roles may include:

* **Community Owner**
* **Community Administrator**
* **Moderator**
* **Member**

The Community Owner shall have the highest level of control within the community, while other roles shall receive permissions according to their assigned responsibilities.

Community roles may control permissions such as:

* Managing members
* Managing channels
* Sending messages
* Editing community information
* Managing roles
* Removing members
* Moderating content
* Managing community settings

#### 3.5.4 Channel Creation

Authorized community members shall be able to create channels based on their assigned permissions.

A channel may contain:

* Channel name
* Channel description
* Channel type
* Visibility configuration
* Channel-specific permissions

Channels may be organized according to different purposes, such as general discussion, announcements, music discussions, or other community activities.

#### 3.5.5 Channel Types

The system may support different channel types.

Possible channel types include:

**Text Channel**
Used for written discussions, messages, replies, reactions, mentions, and other messaging functionality.

**Announcement Channel**
Used by authorized community members to publish important information to community members.

**Private Channel**
Accessible only to selected members or roles.

Additional channel types may be introduced in future versions.

#### 3.5.6 Channel Membership and Access

The system shall verify whether a user has permission to access a channel before returning its content.

Channel access may depend on:

* Community membership
* User role
* Channel permissions
* Private channel membership
* Other access rules

Users without appropriate access shall not be able to retrieve channel messages or protected channel resources through the API.

#### 3.5.7 Channel Messaging

Text channels shall support the messaging functionality defined in Section 3.3.

Users with permission to participate in a channel may be able to:

* Send messages
* Reply to messages
* React to messages
* Mention users
* Create threads
* Edit their own messages
* Delete their own messages where permitted
* Share supported attachments
* View message history

Channel messages shall be delivered in real time to authorized connected users.

#### 3.5.8 Community Information Management

Authorized community administrators shall be able to update community information.

Supported settings may include:

* Community name
* Description
* Community image
* Visibility
* Membership settings
* Default channels
* Other community preferences

Changes shall be validated and saved by the backend.
